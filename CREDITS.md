# Asset credits

## Apollo 11 Command Module exterior

- Source: Smithsonian Institution, National Air and Space Museum
- Object: Command Module, Apollo 11 (CM-107 Columbia)
- Record: https://3d.si.edu/object/3d/command-module-apollo-11%3Ad8c63e8a-4ebc-11ea-b77f-2e728ce88125
- File: Smithsonian 3D API, `multi-mesh2-100k-2048_std_draco.glb`, exterior package `d8c6457e-4ebc-11ea-b77f-2e728ce88125`
- Rights: CC0 / public domain
- Changes: browser-optimized model normalized to a 3.912 m base diameter, centered, lit, and integrated with animated educational reconstruction layers.

## Reconstructed elements

The docking probe, launch escape assembly, cabin X-ray, subsystem layout, RCS plumes, plasma, parachutes, Earth, ocean, telemetry, and mission motion are procedural educational reconstructions. They are not part of the Smithsonian scan.
